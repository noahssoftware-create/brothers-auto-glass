What We Don't Cover

We Are Honest
About Our Limits.

Our warranty covers everything related to how we do the job. What we cannot control is what happens to your vehicle after we leave.

If a rock flies up and cracks your new windshield on the way home, that is not something our warranty can cover. That is a new incident, the same way a collision would be.

For that kind of damage, we are always here to help you get it repaired again quickly and at a fair price.

Not Covered by Warranty

New rock chips, cracks, or any damage caused by road debris, impacts, accidents, or anything outside of our original installation. These are new incidents and not related to our workmanship.

We are always happy to talk through what is and is not covered. Give us a call.
